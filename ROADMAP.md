# SmartProBono Roadmap

This is product direction, not a guarantee of dates.

## Shipped

- guided onboarding
- trusted emergency contact
- Safety Mode
- location capture / share flow
- optional safety recording
- calm guidance
- local session history
- App Store release
- support / privacy web pages

## Improving

- Family Hub and Kid Track QA
- Travel Mode continuity
- permission and recovery handling
- recording reliability
- accessibility
- Health Check diagnostics
- App Store / support-site consistency

## Exploring

- stronger multi-contact coordination
- clearer export / evidence organization
- improved session summaries
- safer cross-device continuity where it can preserve privacy

## Product boundary

SmartProBono is a support and documentation tool. It is not emergency dispatch and does not replace emergency services or individualized legal advice.
